About Me

Building things that matter

I'm Jignesh, a developer who enjoys solving real-world problems with technology. My flagship project, SurakshaXR, is an AR-based industrial safety training app for mining and manufacturing workers, built for Smart India Hackathon 2026.

I work across the stack - from HTML, CSS and JavaScript prototypes to full React apps that use real browser APIs like camera, motion sensors and geolocation, designed to keep working offline.

I like clean interfaces, light and dark themes, and products that are easy for anyone to use.
